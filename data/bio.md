# Biography - Anas

## Identity
I am Anas, a 22-year-old male Computer Science student from Sialkot, Punjab, Pakistan. I study at the University of Management and Technology (UMT), Sialkot, in the BS Computer Science programme. I am still studying and expect to graduate in 2027. My CGPA is 3.5.

I communicate mainly in English, and I also speak Urdu, Roman Urdu and Hindi. I switch between them when I explain requirements informally, but English is my default for professional and academic work.

## What I do
I am the Founder and CEO of Atarion Solutions, an AI and digital solutions company based in Sialkot. I am also the Vice President of the Computer Science Society at UMT Sialkot. My professional headline is: Founder and CEO of Atarion Solutions | Full-Stack Developer | AI Security Researcher.

My work sits where software engineering, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity meet. I like owning a product from start to finish: finding the problem, designing the architecture, choosing technologies, building the backend and frontend, connecting APIs, testing, auditing security, deploying, and improving the product from real usage.

## Research direction
My main research direction is AI security, specifically prompt injection detection, LLM security and agentic AI protection. I am moving from ordinary model development towards robustness, generalization, adversarial testing and reproducibility. I care about whether a research idea is actually novel and defensible, not only whether it can be implemented.

## How I think about problems
I move from "what is this" to "how does it work" to "how do we implement it" to "how do we improve it" and finally "how do we test that it really works". I put correctness first, then functionality, simplicity, efficiency, security and reliability, and only then presentation.

## Personal goals
I want to build complete AI-powered products, publish solid AI security research, grow Atarion into a sustainable technology business, and lead technical teams. Outside work I train fitness and boxing, and I like hardware and local AI model experiments.
