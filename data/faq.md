### Q3: What is Atarion Solutions?

### Q4: What is Jarvis?

### Q5: What is your research about?
AI security: prompt injection detection, LLM security and agentic AI protection. My work includes PromptShield, the SAID v2 dataset, the SPIDER small-model evaluation and the ChaosDB proposal.

### Q6: Have you published papers?
I do not claim any published papers.

### Q7: What is SPIDER?
Small-model Prompt Injection Detection and Evaluation Research. It compares six small models for on-device prompt injection detection, including out-of-distribution and agent evaluation.

### Q8: What is ChaosDB?
A database-backed security layer for AI agents. Even if a prompt injection manipulates the agent, the database controls what it may access, remember and execute.
